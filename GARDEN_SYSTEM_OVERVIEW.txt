1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SkyRealms Garden System is an expansive, progression-driven farming overhaul
designed to replace the legacy physical 5x5 "Farming Generator" mechanic with an
organic, immersive agricultural conservatory called the "Sakura Glass Garden".

Key gameplay pillars:
1. Divine Offering Ritual: Players craft 3 Enchanted Hay Bales, 3 Enchanted Carrots,
   and 3 Enchanted Sugar Cane, placing them into a renamed chest ("Offering to the Gods")
   on their island. This triggers an in-world cinematic featuring an Allay divine emissary
   and permanently unlocks island Garden access.
2. Sakura Glass Garden Conservatory: A unified 520x168 block Japanese botanical
   greenhouse generated inside a dedicated void dimension ("skyrealms_garden"). Features
   curved transparent glass silhouettes, Torii gatehouses, cherry blossom trees,
   a central Japanese garden with koi pond and moon bridge, tea house, waterfall, and
   16 individual 4x4 chunk (64x64 block) crop plots.
3. Elarion Botanical Guide & First Harvest Quest: On first entry, Elarion greets the
   player, presents starter supplies (Novice Hoe, 16 Wheat Seeds, 8 Bone Meal), and
   initiates the First Harvest quest (Prepare 16 Farmland, Plant 16 Seeds, Harvest 16 Wheat).
4. Natural Farming Engine: Instant right-click fast harvesting with auto-replant,
   vanilla left-click break harvesting with maturity verification, anti-exploit
   player-placed block tracking, and real-time action bar progress.
5. Crop Mastery System: 10 individual crops, each with 30 mastery levels following a
   power curve (Math.floor(50 * level^2.1)), 7 claimed milestones per crop awarding
   permanent double-drop chances, active potion buffs, rare drops, and economy coin rewards.
6. Full Administration & Auto-Healing: Administrative controls (/garden admin reset <player>,
   tp, unlockplot, giveitem) and automatic database self-healing for missing plots.

2. PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE & MODULE RELATIONSHIPS
================================================================================
SkyRealms follows a modular Direct Acyclic Graph (DAG) architecture managed by
SkyRealmsCore. Plugins register as modules and expose public API services via Bukkit's
ServicesManager and custom ServiceRegistries.

Module Dependency Chain:
  [platform:api]               -> Shared interfaces, events, and utilities
        |
  [platform:common]            -> Base implementations, math, spatial algorithms
        |
  [platform:database-api]      -> Database pooling & transaction abstractions
        |
  [plugins:SkyRealmsDatabase]  -> HikariCP pool, PostgreSQL / H2 driver, Flyway migrations
        |
  [plugins:SkyRealmsPlayer]    -> Profile persistence, sessions, join listeners
        |
  [plugins:SkyRealmsEconomy]   -> Multi-currency engine (Coins, Gems), Vault hook
        |
  [plugins:SkyRealmsIslands]   -> SkyBlock islands, permissions, island spatial grid
        |
  [plugins:SkyRealmsFarming]   -> Garden system, natural farming engine, crop mastery

     * /garden admin reset <player>: Completely resets an island and player's Garden state:
       - Deletes offering records from sr_garden_offerings.
       - Locks garden access in sr_garden_unlocks.

       - Deletes First Harvest quest progress in sr_farming_quests.

4. garden/Garden.java

   - Fields:
     * gardenId (UUID): Primary key.

     * worldName (String): "skyrealms_garden".
     * centerX, centerY, centerZ (int): Geometric center coordinates (Y=100).
     * size (int): Bounding box footprint (default 768 blocks).
     * unlocked (boolean): Unlock status.

     * minX(), maxX(), minZ(), maxZ(): Bounding box extents.

     * getCenterLocation(): Center Bukkit Location.
     * getWestGateSpawnLocation(): Entrance arrival spawn facing down the Sakura promenade.

5. garden/GardenPlot.java
   - Role: Model representing one of the 16 agricultural plots inside a Garden.
   - Layout:
     * 2 rows of 8 columns.
     * Footprint: 4x4 chunks (64x64 blocks = 4,096 blocks per plot).
     * Overall farming footprint: 512x128 blocks across the conservatory.
     * North row (plots 1 to 8): Z offset -48 blocks.
     * South row (plots 9 to 16): Z offset +48 blocks.
     * Grand Central Boulevard spans Z [-16, +15] (32 blocks wide, 2 chunks).

   - Key Methods:
     * contains(gardenCenterX, gardenCenterZ, bx, bz): Boundary check for plot interior.
     * getCenterX(), getCenterZ(), getMinX(), getMaxX(), getMinZ(), getMaxZ().
     * getCenterLocation(Garden garden): Center spawn location inside the plot.

6. garden/GardenSpatialAllocator.java

   - Methods:
     * indexToGrid(int n): Maps sequential index (0, 1, 2, ...) to 2D spiral (x, z).
     * getGardenCenter(int n): Returns world coordinates (x * 1024, z * 1024).
     * getRegionBounds(int n): Bounding box record for region n.
     * getNearestGridAnchor(blockX, blockZ): Reverses world coordinates to the nearest anchor.

9. garden/GardenService.java & garden/DefaultGardenService.java

     * teleportToGarden(player, islandId): Safe teleport to West Grand Torii Gate arrival dais.
     * teleportToIsland(player, islandId): Safe teleport to player's SkyBlock island.
     * isGardenWorld(world): Validates if world is "skyrealms_garden".

10. garden/NaturalFarmingEngine.java
    - Role: Natural farming engine governing crop planting, growth, maturity, and harvesting.
    - Supported Crops: WHEAT, CARROTS, POTATOES, BEETROOTS, MELON, PUMPKIN, SUGAR_CANE,
      CACTUS, COCOA, NETHER_WART.
    - Maturity Validation (isMature):
      * Ageable crops (Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroots, Cocoa, Nether Wart): age >= maxAge.
      * Stem crops (Melon, Pumpkin): must not be tagged as player-placed.
      * Vertical crops (Sugar Cane, Cactus): must be above an existing stalk & not player-placed.
    - Anti-Exploit System:
      * Fast bit-packed block coordinate keys: (((x & 0x3FFFFFF) << 38) | ((y & 0xFFF) << 26) | (z & 0x3FFFFFF)).
      * Tracks manual block placements in ConcurrentHashMap; clears on natural growth/spread.
      * Prevents placing and re-breaking crops to duplicate XP or mastery.
    - Harvesting Methods:
      * handleRightClickHarvest(player, block):
        - Validates maturity. If immature, sends action bar: "⚠ This crop is not fully grown yet!"
        - Resolves plot (with resilient starter plot fallback inside the garden).
        - Fires GardenCropHarvestEvent.
        - Replants crop automatically (resets age to 0) or clears air.
        - Directly deposits crop drops into player inventory (overflow drops on ground).
        - Awards farming XP, collection count, particles (HAPPY_VILLAGER), and pickup sound.
      * handleBreakHarvest(player, block):
        - Handles manual left-click break.
        - Validates maturity. If immature, sends action bar: "⚠ This crop is not fully grown yet!"
        - Resolves plot (with resilient starter plot fallback inside the garden).
        - Fires GardenCropHarvestEvent.
        - Awards farming XP and collections.

11. garden/NaturalFarmingListener.java
    - Role: Bukkit listener integrating natural farming into Minecraft world events.
    - Events:
      * onPlayerInteract: Intercepts right-clicks on mature crops for instant replant harvest.
      * onBlockBreak: Intercepts normal breaking of garden crops.
      * onBlockPlace: Tags placed stems/canes as player-placed; publishes GardenCropPlantEvent
        when seeds are planted inside unlocked plots.
      * onBlockGrow & onBlockSpread: Clears player-placed tags on natural growth; provides
        bonus chime and accelerated growth for fertile soil.

12. garden/GardenProtectionListener.java
    - Role: Strict protection engine for the Sakura Glass Garden conservatory.
    - Rules:
      * Validates player island membership and BLOCK_BREAK / BLOCK_PLACE permissions.
      * Within plots: Allows crop planting and harvesting only if plot is unlocked.
        If plot is locked, cancels action and informs player: "🔒 Plot #X is locked!"
      * Outside plots: Protects all conservatory architecture (Quartz, Stained Glass,
        Cherry logs/leaves/wood, Torii arches, walkways, Tea House, lamps). Prevents dismantling.
      * Void Boundary Fallback: Intercepts player moves or falls outside region bounds and
        teleports them back safely to the West Torii entrance.

      * Level 20: +15% double drop chance + Rare mutation boosts.
      * Level 25: +15% double drop chance + Coin economy bonus.

5. COMMANDS & ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROLS

Player Commands:
  /garden              -> Teleport to your Greenhouse Dome (alias: /g, /garden home)
  /garden home         -> Teleport to your Greenhouse Dome (checks offering unlock)
  /garden leave        -> Teleport back to your Island spawn
  /garden mastery      -> Open the 10-crop Mastery progression GUI (alias: /garden skills)
  /garden quest        -> View First Harvest quest objectives and current progress
  /garden info         -> View your Garden coordinates and region extents
  /garden plots        -> View the status and crops of all 16 agricultural plots
  /garden help         -> View the Garden command guide

Admin Commands (Permission: skyrealms.garden.admin or Server OP):
  /garden admin tp <player>
    - Teleports admin directly to target player's Garden conservatory.
  /garden admin reset <player>
    - Full progression and ritual reset. Wipes offering history, locks garden access,
      resets Elarion intro, wipes First Harvest quest, and teleports player back to island.
      Player must complete the Divine Offering ritual again.
  /garden admin unlockplot <player> <1-16>
    - Instantly unlocks a specific plot for target player's island garden.
  /garden admin giveitem <player> <hay|carrot|cane|hoe|seeds|bonemeal> [amount]
    - Spawns authentic custom offering items or starter supplies for testing and player assistance.


================================================================================
6. STEP-BY-STEP GAMEPLAY LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

Step 1: The Divine Offering
  - Player crafts:
    * 3x Enchanted Hay Bales
    * 3x Enchanted Carrots
    * 3x Enchanted Sugar Cane
  - Player places them in a chest renamed: "Offering to the Gods" on their island.
  - OfferingListener detects the submission and initiates DefaultOfferingService.
  - Items are consumed atomically. Concurrency lock prevents duplicate submissions.

Step 2: The Divine Arrival Cinematic
  - A thunderstorm crackles (visual only, no fire).
  - An Allay Divine Emissary spawns, floating in a column of sacred light and cherry petals.
  - Subtitles explain that the Botanical Gods are pleased with the island's tribute.
  - Permanent access to the Sakura Conservatory is granted and saved in the database.

Step 3: Entering the Conservatory & Meeting Elarion
  - Player runs /garden home.
  - Player arrives at the West Grand Torii Gate arrival pavilion inside skyrealms_garden.
  - Elarion the Botanist greets the player, welcomes them beneath the glass dome, and gives:
    * Novice Hoe (Unbreakable)
    * 16x Wheat Seeds
    * 8x Bone Meal
  - The "First Harvest" quest begins automatically.

Step 4: The First Harvest Quest
  - Objective 1: Prepare 16 Farmland (tilling dirt in Plot 1 with the Novice Hoe).
  - Objective 2: Plant 16 Wheat Seeds in Plot 1.
  - Objective 3: Grow and harvest 16 mature golden wheat.
  - Action bar shows live progress on every tilled, planted, or harvested crop.
  - On completion: +500 Farming XP, +2,500 Coins, +16 Carrots, challenge toast fanfare.

Step 5: Crop Mastery Progression & Plot Expansion
  - Every harvested crop awards global Farming XP and specific Crop Mastery XP.
  - Action bar shows: +10 Farming XP | +10 Wheat Mastery [Lvl 1 - 50%]
  - Reaching milestone levels (1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30) unlocks permanent perks:
    double drop rates, speed/haste/night-vision buffs, rare golden crops, coin rewards.
  - Players type /garden mastery to inspect their progress and claim rewards.
  - Players type /garden plots to unlock and cultivate plots 2 through 16!
